Common questions

Everything you might be wondering about Spent.

Is Spent really free?
Yes — 100% free with every feature unlocked. No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalls and no hidden upgrades. We don't sell your data because we never collect it.
What happens if I delete the app?
Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes your data with it. Export a backup first if you want to keep your history before uninstalling or switching phones.
Does it work offline?
Always. Spent is offline-first by design — there's no server to talk to. Tracking, AI insights, receipt scanning and everything else run fully on your device, with or without a connection.
How does AI work without internet?
All AI runs on-device. Insights, categorization and receipt OCR are processed locally using your phone's own hardware, so nothing is ever sent to the cloud.
Is receipt data safe?
Completely. Receipts are scanned with on-device OCR and stored encrypted on your phone. Images and extracted data never leave the device.
